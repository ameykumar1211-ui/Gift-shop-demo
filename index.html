Hands‑free accessibility prototype

Control this app using only your eyes.

EyeAccess uses the front camera to estimate where you are looking. After one short hands‑free calibration, gaze can move a cursor, dwell to select, scroll, and type on the built‑in keyboard.

👁️ 9‑point auto calibrationLook at each target; no repeated tapping needed.
✨ Adaptive smoothingStable when resting, faster when your gaze moves.
⏱️ Dwell to selectHold your gaze on a button to activate it.
⌨️ Gaze keyboardType without touching the screen.
Important: Camera permission requires one initial user/caregiver action in the browser. For full Android‑wide control, including other apps and true background operation, this web prototype must be converted to a native Android Accessibility Service.
Gaze processing is performed on the device by the eye‑tracking library. This page does not upload camera frames to a server.
